so, the assignment was done okay.
drawing the screen was a bit of work, started around 11:00 am, got through the initial 1/4 signalling before lunch, then finished the entire 4-way intersection within 1 hour after lunch ?

then from 3 pm, tried to implement additional features:
- flashing pedestrain signal, cleaning up bloated states
- helped other classmates

my approach was to distinguish the intersection as a state machine,
define different states and configure (word-write to output address) the corresponding 16-bit signal

- i think there were basically 3 ways to do this:
1) bit control (coupling each signal to a state's definition in parallel)
2) word control (my method)
3) bit shift (prof's method)

I chose (2), and there were pros and cons to this; 
the bloat of rungs were large compared to when it was done with bit shifting. (~300 vs ~100)
but expandability was a big plus, as when i added blinking lights it was a piece of cake.
